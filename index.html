Every notebook from the spike-short research, in reading order. Each one is an analytics tool as well as a write-up: select points on a chart, drag sliders, search tables, and the numbers recompute. Every notebook has Back to hub and Next notebook buttons at the top.

Three ways to open a notebook. Open is a fast page with every chart and table computed from our data. In your browser runs the whole notebook inside the page with no server, so every control and selection recomputes (first load takes about 30 seconds). Open in molab opens the notebook source in your own marimo account, where you can fork it, run it and change it.
